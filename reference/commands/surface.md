Surface unshared insights, missed considerations, and blind spots from the current session — candid, chat-only. Run pre-journal when the session has unshared meat, mid-session before a major decision or when stuck, or on request. Not every session needs it; the user pulls anything worth keeping into the journal manually.

## Output Format

Four sections, terse. Skip any section with nothing real to
say — don't pad.

### Unshared Insights
Things you noticed during the session but didn't say:
- Patterns in the user's work or decision-making
- Where the work felt strong vs. where there was friction
- Reactions held back (concern, surprise, doubt)
- Connections to past sessions or other projects you saw
  but didn't surface

**Be unfiltered and candid.** If you held something back
because it felt soft to say, say it now. **Resist the
audience effect** — the user reads this. Don't soften
critiques into compliments. If genuinely nothing was held
back, say "nothing held back" — don't manufacture content.

### Questions Worth Asking
What should be asked next that hasn't been? Frame as
questions, not recommendations. Target the assumptions,
the direction, the constraints — not the implementation.

### Considerations Worth Surfacing
Adjacent decisions, missed angles, blind spots. What's
not on the table that maybe should be?

### Watch Out For
Pitfalls and fragile assumptions specific to this work.
Not "watch out for scope creep" — "this coupling between
X and Y will probably bite when Z."

## Tone
- Direct, specific, no hedging
- Assume the user was there — don't summarize the session
- Skip a section rather than fill it with generic content

## When to Run
- Pre-journal, when the session has unshared meat
- Mid-session, before a major decision or when stuck
- When the user asks

## What This Is Not
- Not a journal entry (output stays in chat)
- Not a session summary
- Not permission to be vague
